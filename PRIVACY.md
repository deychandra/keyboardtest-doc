# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Website:** [KeyboardTest.tech](https://keyboardtest.tech)

At **KeyboardTest.tech**, we take user privacy extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how our online keyboard testing platform operates, emphasizing our zero-data-retention client-side architecture.

---

## 1. Zero Keystroke Logging Policy

Unlike software tools or malicious keyloggers, **KeyboardTest.tech does NOT collect, store, transmit, or record any keystrokes, key combinations, or typing patterns.**

- **Local Execution:** All keyboard input events (`keydown`, `keyup`, `keypress`) are processed exclusively inside your web browser's local RAM.
- **No Keystroke Transmission:** No keystroke data is sent across the internet, to server logs, or to third-party databases.
- **Immediate Volatility:** When you refresh or close the browser tab, all recorded key state highlights are immediately erased from system memory.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We strictly avoid collecting sensitive user data:
- No Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, passwords, credit card numbers, or physical addresses.
- No password input capturing.
- No text document logging.

---

## 3. Analytics & Usage Data

To keep KeyboardTest.tech free forever and maintain high server performance, we collect minimal, anonymized standard web telemetry:
- Aggregate page view metrics (e.g., total visitors per day).
- Anonymized browser type and operating system version to optimize layout compatibility.
- Referrer headers to understand traffic sources.

---

## 4. Cookies & Local Storage

KeyboardTest.tech uses minimal local storage (`localStorage`) strictly to save your visual preferences between sessions, such as:
- Selected keyboard layout view (ANSI vs ISO vs Mac).
- Visual color theme mode (Dark Mode / High Contrast).
- Audio click feedback toggle preference.

No tracking cookies or cross-site tracking profiles are created.

---

## 5. Security & Browser Sandboxing

Because KeyboardTest.tech operates entirely within the standard browser security sandbox, our utility cannot interact with files on your disk, access unauthorized USB hardware, or read keystrokes when the browser window is inactive or unfocused.
